## 2. EXACTLY-ONCE STORE COMMIT PROOF

To prevent duplicate store execution across consecutive backpressure clock cycles:
- **`LATENCY = 0`**: `tx_committed` register latches `1` upon store commit and prevents re-committing the same store while `ex_mem_reg` holds the instruction in EX/MEM.
- **`LATENCY > 0`**: Store commit occurs **EXACTLY ONCE** in `STATE_RESPONSE` when `dmem_resp_ready` is asserted. `dmem_state` then transitions to `STATE_IDLE` on `posedge clk`.
